NextBody is the companion app for the HOOP band. It helps you read your body's daily state and keep track of food and training.

KNOW IF TODAY'S A GO
Wake up to a 0-100 readiness score, with a breakdown of how last night's sleep, awake fatigue, training and physiological strain each moved it.

SEE YOUR SLEEP CLEARLY
Duration, efficiency, restfulness, REM, deep sleep, bedtime and routine regularity. Seven sleep factors, each with its own score bar and explanation.

TRAIN AROUND RECOVERY
Training load builds up through the day, and your suggested range moves with your recovery. Easy days count too.

ALL-DAY STRESS
The band reads stress around the clock. See when it rises, how high it gets and how long recovery takes.

FOOD LOG
Log meals and see the day's calories, protein, carbs and fat, and how much is left. You can also connect your own AI assistant to log a meal into today with one sentence.

BODY COMPOSITION
Rest a finger on the band's side key for about 30 seconds to get 14 estimates, including body fat, lean mass, muscle and body water.

Some measurements and logging require a compatible HOOP band and the relevant permissions.

NextBody provides general wellness and fitness information. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or replace professional medical advice.
